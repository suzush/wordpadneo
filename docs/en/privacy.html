Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

1. General Policy

WordPad Neo is an open-source document editing application for Windows. The developer of WordPad Neo does not collect users' personal information through the application. Document contents and usage data are not transmitted to servers operated by the developer.

2. Collection of Personal Information

WordPad Neo does not include features intended to collect personal information such as names, addresses, email addresses, or phone numbers on behalf of the developer. The application does not track users for advertising purposes or perform user behavior analytics.

3. Handling of Document Data

WordPad Neo accesses documents selected by users solely for the purpose of displaying, editing, and saving their contents. Even if documents contain personal information, the developer does not collect that information.

Documents are saved to locations selected by users. If documents are saved in folders synchronized with services such as OneDrive, the privacy policies and settings of those services apply.

4. Data Transmission and Sharing

WordPad Neo does not transmit users' personal information or document contents to external servers operated by the developer. The developer does not sell or share such information with advertisers or other third parties.

5. This Website

This website consists of static HTML and CSS. It does not use its own analytics services, advertising trackers, or tracking cookies. However, GitHub Pages, which hosts this website, may process connection information and other data in accordance with GitHub's privacy policies. For more information, please refer to the GitHub Privacy Statement .

6. Third-Party Services

Information processed by third-party services, including GitHub, Microsoft Store, and Windows, is subject to the respective privacy policies of those services.

7. Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us through GitHub Issues . Please do not include personal or confidential information in public issues.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes in the application's functionality or operations. Any updates will be published on this page.
